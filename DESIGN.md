# Ar Rahmah Malaysia: website mockup design direction

Client: Pertubuhan Ar Rahmah Malaysia (NGO, ROS PMM-022-07-140322013).
Source: their 2 decks (Booklet Profile + Kertas Cadangan Penaung Dakwah).

## Who visits
1. Donors on phone (from FB / WhatsApp link). They want to know: is this real, where does money go, how to pay.
2. Big sponsors (Penaung Dakwah: corporate, yayasan). They want: credibility, numbers, reports, a person to call.
3. Admins (2+ people) who post news, photos and quarterly reports.

## Direction
- Feels like an annual report, not a startup page. Calm, trustworthy, photo-led.
- Real photos from the field do the work. No stock, no illustration.
- Colours from the brand: deep navy `#0A2A55` and gold `#E9B824` (used sparingly: one accent, rules, numbers). Warm paper background `#FAF7F0`.
- Type: Newsreader (serif, headings and big numbers) + Public Sans (body). No Inter/Poppins.
- Malay first. English only for the tagline line.
- No gradients, no glass, no emoji, no icon on every heading. Square-ish corners (4px).
- Layout: text column + photo rows, a real table for centres, a plain bar chart for spending. Few cards.

## Mobile
- Base width 360px. Sticky header with menu button. Sticky "Sumbang" bar at bottom on phones.
- Tap targets 44px+, inputs 16px.

## Pages in this mockup
- `index.html` public home (all key sections)
- `penaung.html` Penaung Dakwah programme + interest form
- `admin.html` custom CMS preview (news, reports, enquiries, multiple admins)
